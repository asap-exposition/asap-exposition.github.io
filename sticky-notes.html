A Short History of the Internet

The internet began as a collection of experimental computer networks designed to allow researchers to communicate and share information. What started as a relatively small academic project eventually became one of the most important communication systems in human history.

As the web became publicly accessible, websites transformed from collections of documents into interactive spaces where people could publish, discuss and collaborate. Today, almost every aspect of digital life depends on this infrastructure.

A collaborative web

Collaborative tools add another layer to the web: instead of simply reading a page, visitors can leave information for one another directly on the page itself.
